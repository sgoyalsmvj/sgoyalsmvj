## 👋 Hey, I'm Saurabh

Full-stack engineer at **Sonata Software**, Bangalore. About two years in, and before that a **founding engineer at Quickads.ai**, where I owned things end to end instead of one ticket at a time.

I'm not an "AI coder." I'm an engineer who **uses AI the way a carpenter uses a power tool**: it's faster, but I still decide where the cut goes. I read the code it writes, I know why it works, and when it doesn't, I can find the bug myself.

## 🧠 How I Work

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 1. Think
Understand the problem before opening the editor. Data shape, failure modes, what happens at 10x load.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 2. Build
By hand when the problem is subtle. With AI when it's repetitive or I already know exactly what I want.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 3. Verify
Nothing ships because "the model wrote it." Tests, logs, and reading every diff.

</td>
</tr>

## 🎓 What I Believe

> **Understand it, then ship it.**

- **Reading code is the skill that matters now.** Anyone can generate it. Knowing why it's right or wrong is the job.
- **Fast is only useful if it's correct.** I use AI to move quicker, never to skip the thinking.
- **Ship, then learn.** A launched thing with real feedback beats a perfect thing in stealth.
- **Share what I figure out.** Open source is how I learned most of what I know.
